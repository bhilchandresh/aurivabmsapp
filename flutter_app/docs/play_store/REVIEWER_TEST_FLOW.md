# AurivaBMS Reviewer Test Flow

The following is a recommended testing journey for Google Play Store reviewers to verify core app functionality:

1. **Install AurivaBMS** from the provided testing track/bundle.
2. **Open application** and arrive at the Login screen.
3. **Login** using the provided test account credentials.
4. **Dashboard**: Verify the loading of high-level business stats (revenue, pending amounts, quick actions).
5. **Clients**: Navigate to the Clients section to view the list of saved clients and view a client's details.
6. **Invoices**: Navigate to the Invoices section. View existing invoices and click on an invoice to view the generated PDF/Preview.
7. **Quotations**: Navigate to the Quotations section and view a sample quotation.
8. **Expenses**: Navigate to the Expenses module to see categorized business expenses.
9. **Staff / Team**: View the team directory (if accessible by the test account role).
10. **Inventory**: View the product catalog and stock levels.
11. **Suppliers**: View supplier details and purchase histories.
12. **Settings**: Navigate to the Settings tab to view business profile configuration and GST toggles.
13. **Logout**: Safely log out of the application from the Settings/Profile screen.
