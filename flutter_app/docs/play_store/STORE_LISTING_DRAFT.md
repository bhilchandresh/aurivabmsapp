## 1. App Name
AurivaBMS

## 2. Short Description
A complete Business Management System for managing invoices, quotations, clients, and inventory.

## 3. Full Description
AurivaBMS is a comprehensive Business Management System designed to streamline your daily operations. From creating professional invoices to managing client relationships and tracking expenses, AurivaBMS provides the tools you need to stay organized and efficient.

## 4. Key Features
- **Invoice Management**: Create, edit, and generate PDFs for invoices effortlessly.
- **Quotation Management**: Draft and share professional quotations with clients.
- **Client Management**: Maintain a central directory of your clients.
- **Supplier Management**: Keep track of your suppliers and purchase histories.
- **Expense Tracking**: Log and monitor business expenses in real-time.
- **Inventory Management**: Oversee stock levels and product catalogs.
- **Staff/Team Management**: Manage your team directory securely.
- **Dashboard & Analytics**: View business performance, revenues, and pending balances at a glance.

## 5. Business Use Cases
Ideal for small to medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), freelancers, and service providers looking for a centralized platform to manage billing, clients, and core business data directly from their mobile devices.
