# AurivaBMS Store Graphics Checklist

- **App Icon**
  - Required: YES
  - Status: [REQUIRES USER ACTION]
  - Dimensions: 512px by 512px (PNG or JPEG)
  - Remaining action: Upload to Play Console.

- **Feature Graphic**
  - Required: YES
  - Status: [REQUIRES USER ACTION]
  - Dimensions: 1024px by 500px (PNG or JPEG)
  - Remaining action: Upload to Play Console.

- **Phone Screenshots**
  - Required: YES (Minimum 2, up to 8)
  - Status: [REQUIRES USER ACTION]
  - Dimensions: 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio (Min 320px, Max 3840px)
  - Remaining action: Capture screens of Dashboard, Invoices, Clients, etc. Upload to Play Console.

- **Tablet Screenshots (7-inch and 10-inch)**
  - Required: YES (If supporting tablets natively)
  - Status: [REQUIRES USER ACTION]
  - Dimensions: Same rules as phone.
  - Remaining action: Capture tablet-sized screens or use standard screens. Upload to Play Console.
