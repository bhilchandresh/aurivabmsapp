# AurivaBMS Privacy Policy Requirements

The following information must be covered in your Privacy Policy based on the implemented functionality:

- **Account information**: COLLECTED (Required for authentication)
- **Authentication information**: COLLECTED (JWT/Bearer tokens securely stored)
- **Business information**: COLLECTED (Stored on backend via API)
- **Client/customer information**: COLLECTED (Managed within the app)
- **Invoice information**: COLLECTED
- **Quotation information**: COLLECTED
- **Expense information**: COLLECTED
- **Staff/team information**: COLLECTED
- **Supplier information**: COLLECTED
- **Inventory information**: COLLECTED
- **Device information**: COLLECTED (Device token used for push notifications)
- **Notifications**: COLLECTED (OneSignal/Firebase push tokens used)
- **Camera/media access**: COLLECTED (Used for uploading documents/profile pictures locally before API upload)
- **Analytics**: COLLECTED (Firebase Analytics present in dependencies)
- **Crash reporting**: COLLECTED (Firebase Crashlytics present in dependencies)
- **Any identifiers**: COLLECTED (User IDs, Device IDs)
- **API/server communication**: SHARED (Data is transmitted to `https://api.aurivabms.in/api/v1`)

**Note**: You must host a Privacy Policy URL covering these categories to remain compliant with Google Play Store policies.
